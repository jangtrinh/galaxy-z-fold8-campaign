# Campaign asset contract

## Product invariants

Same Galaxy Z Fold8 device in every image: the new compact wide-format Samsung foldable, Graphite colorway, cool dark titanium frame, rounded-rectangle 5.5-inch 10:16 cover screen, 7.6-inch 4:3 inner display, slim central hinge, two vertically stacked rear camera circles, no invented ports or buttons. Identical frame geometry, camera placement, hinge construction, screen bezels, materials, and color in every image. Use the official Samsung product image and the previous approved campaign image as identity references. Never render logos, UI text, labels, or marketing copy into the image.

## Eight assets

1. `hero-open`: full-screen hero, device half-open like a book, low three-quarter angle; right two-thirds; flat acid-coral chroma background for cutout.
2. `view-folded`: product switcher, relaxed folded portrait, full device with padding; flat acid-coral chroma background.
3. `view-hinge`: tight hinge/detail crop; flat acid-coral chroma background.
4. `ratio-motion`: horizontal story card, device mid-unfold with controlled motion cues; flat acid-coral chroma background.
5. `ratio-rest`: first story card, device resting partly open at 90 degrees; flat acid-coral chroma background.
6. `commerce-ghost`: device alone, open 4:3, floating upright; flat acid-coral chroma background.
7. `system-exploded`: wide top-down exploded device system on dark technical surface; no text.
8. `finale-macro`: wide macro of graphite hinge, titanium edge, and luminous display seam; dark center-safe composition.

## Lighting and background

Campaign cutouts share hard studio key light, deep contrast, cool titanium edge light, and one warm coral bounce. Chroma background is perfectly flat and absent from the product, with no floor line, shadow, gradient, reflection, or texture.

## Avoid list

Wrong product proportions, extra cameras, altered hinge, invented controls, duplicated device parts, text, logos, watermarks, floating labels, generic slab phone, rollable screen, transparent hardware, hands, people, props, floor, cast shadow.
